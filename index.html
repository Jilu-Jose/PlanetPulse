Methodology & Sources

How PlanetPulse calculates your carbon footprint.

Core Calculation Formula

CO₂e = Quantity × Emission Factor

Every emission estimate in PlanetPulse is computed using this deterministic formula. The quantity you enter (e.g., kilometers driven, grams of food consumed, kWh of electricity used) is multiplied by a peer-reviewed emission factor from a trusted scientific source.

Carbon calculations are deterministic. AI only explains results; it never calculates them.

The AI assistant in PlanetPulse provides natural-language explanations, comparisons, and recommendations — but every CO₂e number you see is calculated by our verified calculation engine using published emission factors, not by the AI model.

Uncertainty & Estimates

Figures marked "Estimate" carry ±15–25% uncertainty depending on regional grid mix, vehicle load, and food provenance.

PlanetPulse uses global average emission factors where regional data is unavailable. For more accurate results, connect your smart meter or enter your regional electricity tariff in Settings. All emission factors are reviewed annually and updated when new IPCC or DEFRA data is published.
